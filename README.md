# Counter - Factorio Mod

A powerful factory analysis tool that counts and categorizes everything in your selected area or blueprint.

## Features

- **Area Selection**: Select any area on your map to instantly count all entities, tiles, fluids, items, and more
- **Blueprint Analysis**: Load any blueprint to see exactly what resources and components it contains
- **Comprehensive Counting**:
  - Entities and structures
  - Tiles and terrain
  - Items in inventories
  - Fluids in tanks and pipes
  - Equipment in grids
  - Items on belts and inserters
  - Wires and circuit connections
  - Virtual and item signals

- **Organized GUI**: Results displayed in a clean, tabbed interface with categories for Surface, Inventories, Belt Systems, and Wires & Signals

## Usage

* **Area Selection**: Click the Counter shortcut to activate the selection tool, then select an area on your map.
* **Blueprint Analysis**: Click the Counter shortcut while holding a blueprint in your cursor to instantly count and analyze everything contained within it.

Perfect for factory planning, resource analysis, and understanding complex builds!
